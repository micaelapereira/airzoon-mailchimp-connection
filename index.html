Connect Mailchimp to the airZoon panel

Clients can connect their own Mailchimp account. airZoon sends the contacts it collects to Mailchimp, and clients can bring their Mailchimp contacts into airZoon. This page explains the design and the two options for the Dashboard.

Open prototype · Option A Open prototype · Option B

What clients can do

Connect

One button. The client signs in to Mailchimp and allows airZoon. No keys to copy.

Send contacts

Contacts with an email go to the client's Mailchimp audience. Automatic, and also on demand with "Send contacts to Mailchimp".

Import contacts

The client brings subscribed people from Mailchimp into Contacts, after confirming they agreed to get emails. They can do it again at any time.

Where it lives in the panel

The feature touches three places. Only the Dashboard has two options. The rest is the same in both.

My information

Where the client connects, changes settings, sends, imports and disconnects. A Mailchimp block in the right column, under Status. It is visible without scrolling.

Contacts

A Source column (airZoon or Mailchimp) that never changes, a Mailchimp column (in Mailchimp, not sent, unsubscribed) and a filter by source. Three number cards on top: collected by airZoon, in your Mailchimp, and imported from Mailchimp.

Dashboard

Keeps Mailchimp in view: invites to connect, shows the status, warns when something needs attention or a send stops. Every link goes to My information.

Two options for the Dashboard

Both show the same information and the same states. They only differ in where they sit and how much space they take.
